Our story

Straightforward work, done right.

Decks Plus is run by Allen Richards, building decks, patios, fire pits, pergolas, outdoor kitchens, and custom outdoor structures for homeowners across Hamilton County, Kokomo, and Central Indiana. From framing a deck to rebuilding a waterfront dock, the goal is the same: solid work that holds up, built by someone who shows up and does what he says.

Every project gets walked through in person before it starts — layout, materials, and budget — so there are no surprises once the crew shows up with lumber.
