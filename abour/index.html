Decks Plus is an owner-operated deck and outdoor living business based in Kokomo, Indiana.

Our story

Straightforward work, done right.

Decks Plus is run by Allen Richards, building decks, patios, fire pits, pergolas, and custom outdoor structures across Kokomo and Central Indiana. From framing a simple deck to rebuilding a waterfront dock, the goal is the same: solid work that holds up, built by someone who shows up and does what he says.

Every project gets walked through in person before it starts — layout, materials, and budget — so there are no surprises once the crew shows up with lumber.
